About us

A simple idea, taken seriously

Sunvibes Tech Solutions was created to build useful applications for everyday life and everyday work, with a particular focus on health, care, and custom solutions built around each professional's or business's specific needs.

Our founder holds a degree in clinical psychology and has a background spanning clinical research at international pharmaceutical companies and public healthcare systems, in Italy and abroad. He brings together an understanding of people and their needs with technology — experience that led directly to our first product: a simple way to find the nearest emergency room, wherever you are.

Our first product is Pronto? Soccorso Vicino ("Emergency? Nearby help"). Others will follow, with the same principle: do one useful thing well, before promising ten.

Complicated work, reduced to one click

Every practice, every business has its own way of working — and software built to suit everyone often doesn't adapt to that, it complicates it. We build custom management tools and apps around how you actually work, from your starting idea to needs you hadn't even considered yet. Not an off-the-rack suit altered to roughly fit: a bespoke suit, tailored to your exact measurements.

  • Built around how you work, not the other way around
  • One click instead of ten manual steps
  • Designed for needs you hadn't even considered yet

A concrete example: an appointment-booking system for a medical practice or a sales business, turning a process that's cumbersome today into a one-click operation. Another example: a customer app (or an internal management tool) for pharmacies, to book medication pickups, send automatic reminders, and stay in touch with the people who rely on you every day.
